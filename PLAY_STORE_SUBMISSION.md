## Full description (2940 characters; maximum 4,000)

A little care, every day.

Wellbeing is a private, offline journal for adults who want a simple way to record meals, hydration, movement and everyday wellbeing. Choose English, Sinhala or Tamil, switch between light and dark appearance, and build a routine at your own pace.

FOOD, WITH YOU IN CONTROL
Search a small reference library of everyday foods or enter the energy information from your own package label. Review the food and amount actually eaten before saving. Calories are calculated from the confirmed portion; reference foods also show protein, carbohydrate, fat and fibre. Edit or delete entries whenever needed.

The reference library contains eight foods with source identifiers and versioned USDA-derived data. Values are checked against the included source records, not generated by AI. Food preparation and portions vary, so these values remain estimates. The library is not a complete Sri Lankan food database and has not received clinical or dietitian approval.

YOUR EVERYDAY JOURNAL
Record water, optional mood check-ins, weight and manual activities. Use a walking, workout or stretching timer with pause and resume. See recent measurements and seven-day water and activity summaries. Days without records are clearly marked as not logged.

GENTLE, OPTIONAL REMINDERS
Choose a daily reminder time, snooze a notification or turn reminders off. Quiet hours run from 21:00 to 08:00. Delivery is approximate and can be delayed by Android battery settings. Notification permission is requested only when you choose to enable reminders, and messages never include personal health values.

FAMILIAR RECIPE IDEAS
Explore three simple recipe ideas and filter declared allergens and cuisine. Check ingredient packaging and cross-contact risks. Recipe nutrition and current retail prices are not verified; costs are not invented.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
No account, advertising, analytics or automatic cloud uploads. Journal contents and the local change queue are encrypted on your device using Android Keystore. Export your journal to a JSON file you control, or delete your profile and journal in the app. Exported files remain wherever you saved them; a cloud document provider may upload a file if you choose that destination.

Cloud synchronization is not active. Photo recognition, barcode scanning, Health Connect and clinical report interpretation are not included.

GENERAL WELLNESS ONLY
Wellbeing is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified health professional for medical advice. Nutrition estimates and the optional BMI screening number are not medical prescriptions. BMI is hidden for ages 18–19 and can be disabled when unsuitable. If you follow a clinician's fluid restriction, follow that plan.

For adults aged 18 and over. No pressure, punitive streaks or automatic calorie targets—just a clearer view of the habits you choose to record.

### Purpose

Wellbeing is an offline general-wellness journal for adults. It supports manual food, water, activity, weight and optional mood records, preferences and optional local reminders. It is not a medical device or medical service and does not provide diagnoses or treatment.

### Information you choose to record

The app stores age for adult eligibility; optional height, BMI suitability, fluid-restriction and allergen preferences; food names, portions, sources and nutrition estimates; water, weight, activity and mood records; timestamps; and active timer state. Language, appearance and reminder settings are also stored locally. Optional fields can be skipped. No account, email, exact birth date, location, photographs, contacts or payment information is required.

### Storage and use

Journal contents and pending local changes are encrypted in the app's private Room database with an Android Keystore key. The encrypted change queue is a foundation for possible future synchronization; no provider is connected and no uploads occur. Non-sensitive language, appearance and reminder preferences are stored as local settings. Records are used to display your journal, calculate confirmed-entry totals and show history. Requested reminders use only your reminder settings, not private health values. No advertising, analytics or automatic diagnostics service is integrated.

Backup and device transfer are excluded. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's journal and key access. Keep your device locked. Encryption does not protect information visible on an unlocked screen or readable files you export.

### Notifications

Reminders are optional and initially disabled. Android 13+ requests notification permission only when you choose to enable them. Messages use generic journal prompts, not personal values. Change the time, snooze or disable them in the app, or revoke permission in Android settings. Delivery can be delayed by Android battery management. Quiet hours run from 21:00 to 08:00 in the device's local timezone.

### Export and disclosure

You can export readable JSON through Android's document picker. Choose a trusted destination and protect the file. If you choose a cloud document provider, it may upload/process that file under its own terms. In-app deletion does not remove exported copies or files you share. This app does not send your journal to publisher servers or a connected cloud service.

### Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall. Individual deletion removes the visible entry and replaces unsent changes with a deletion marker that does not contain the entry's content. Whole-journal deletion clears the profile, records, timer and pending changes and disables reminders. Some non-health preferences such as language/appearance remain until app storage is cleared. We do not claim forensic erasure of physical storage. No publisher-held account or backup exists in this offline version. Manage exported copies at their saved destinations.

### Choices and rights

View, edit, export and delete local records in the app. Contact `[SUPPORT_EMAIL]` for privacy questions and applicable legal requests. Food values are source-referenced estimates; recipe content and translations need appropriate specialist/native-speaker review before broad release. Consult a qualified health professional for medical advice and follow clinician-provided fluid restrictions or other care plans.
